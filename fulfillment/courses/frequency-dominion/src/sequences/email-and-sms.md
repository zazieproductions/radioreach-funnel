written to the artist (you), from purchase day forward.

Subject: Your Frequency Dominion classroom is live
The Vault is the list. Module 01 is identity. Open the classroom, write the sentence, send nobody until it is sharp. #/course/classroom

**Day 1 — War room**
Subject: 20 stations. Not 2,000.
Filter hometown. Score 0–5. Load the war room. Volume without scoring is how you train yourself that radio does not work.

**Day 3 — First blood**
Subject: 10 emails today or the campaign is fake
90-word pitch. Named human. One ask. Log every send. Silence is data.

**Day 8 — Touch 2**
Subject: Do not 'just bump this'
New information only: 30-second hook or a date. Touch 2 is respect. Nagging is how wells burn.

**Day 21 — Audit**
Subject: Adds, interviews, or a honest no
Close the 21-day loop. Thank champions. Clone the war room for market two. Dominion is a calendar, not a mood.
