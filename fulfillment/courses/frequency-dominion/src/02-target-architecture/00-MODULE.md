# MODULE 02 — TARGET ARCHITECTURE

> Build a city-by-city ladder so every send has a reason.

Module 02 turns geography into a plan: heat map, tour pre-roll, 0–5 scoring, and the 20-station war room you can actually work.

| # | Lesson | Spoken | Drill |
|---|--------|:------:|-------|
| 01 | [Hometown heat map](01-hometown-heat-map.md) | ~9 min | Extract 25 hometown contacts from the Vault. Dedupe against your send history. Pre-rank A/B/C. |
| 02 | [Tour-route pre-roll](02-tour-route-pre-roll.md) | ~8 min | Map real dates — or book the invented 4-city weekend — onto D-21 send days in the route planner. |
| 03 | [Format fit scoring](03-format-fit-scoring.md) | ~8 min | Score all 25 rows. 4–5s to the top (day-one batch). CUT the 0–1s with a one-line reason. |
| 04 | [The 20-station war room](04-the-20-station-war-room.md) | ~8 min | Finalize the 20, print the war room, put it on the wall, photo it to your accountability human. |
