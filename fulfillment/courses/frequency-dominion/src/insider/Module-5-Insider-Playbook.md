## THE RELATIONSHIP LADDER (MEMORIZE THE RUNGS)

Radio relationships move in ladder rungs. Every interaction should climb one rung — never skip, never stall:

| Rung | Name | Definition | Your move |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stranger** | No reply, no contact | The pitch |
| 2 | **Contact** | A reply — any reply | Answer fast, offer the call |
| 3 | **Conversation** | A 12-minute call | Listen 3 min, one ask, one next step |
| 4 | **Ally** | An add + thank-you + asset | Thank-you economy |
| 5 | **Champion** | Re-adds, upgrades, introduces you | Value drops, 2-way intros |
| 6 | **Confidant** | Shares feedback, format info, goes to bat | Monthly one-line check-ins, zero asks |

The ladder is why Module 5's cadence exists: a call doesn't get you a champion; the **year** of correct stewardship does. Most artists stall at rung 3 (one call, then nothing) and call radio a closed door.

## THE THANK-YOU MATRIX (WHAT LABELS ACTUALLY DO FOR THEIR CHAMPIONS)

The gesture hierarchy, keyed to rung:

- **Rung 4 (add):** specific 3-day thank-you + social clip tagging the station.
- **Rung 5 (upgrade/repeat):** 2-week asset (station ID, premiere, reserved tickets) + an on-air shout-out offer.
- **Rung 6 (champion):** 90-day value drop — *no ask attached*. Introduce them to an artist. Send a listener for the request line. Give them the story first.

The matrix's rule: **the gesture is always one rung above the ask.** If you want champion behavior, give confidant respect first. This inversion is the entire economy.

## THE 12-MINUTE CALL: THE INSIDER SCRIPT QUEUE

Beyond the structure, the *sequence of questions* does the heavy lifting:

1. **The warm-up is bait, not small talk.** "What's the most exciting thing that's happened on [show] this month?" Their answer is a gift: it tells you exactly what they're proud of — and what to reference forever after.
2. **The format question is a market scan.** "What does a new record need to do to fit here?" The answer contains their internal criteria — *their wording* — which you can mirror in every future pitch.
3. **The timing question is a calendar unlock.** "When's your add cycle and what day does rotation go out?" Answers set your next campaign's dates.
4. **The ask is the smallest possible thing.** One slot. One show. One recheck date. The call's goal is the *next step*, not the commitment.

**Golden rule:** never end a call without a next step and a date. No next step = no relationship. The 12-minute call that ends in a date becomes rung 3 → rung 4 in 72 hours.

## THE INDUSTRY ETIQUETTE CODE (VIOLATIONS THAT BURN WELLS)

- **Never pitch another record in the same conversation as a thank-you.** The thank-you is its own message. Momentum is not a coupon.
- **Never ask a PD for a separate favor (press, playlist, social) in the same ask.** Radio owes you one thing per cycle. One ask, one cycle.
- **Never argue about a "no."** A "not now" is a calendar answer, not a judgment. A polite "understood, checking back in [month]" converts a no into a recheck — which is a win disguised as a rejection.
- **Never use the words "sorry to bother you" or "I know you're busy."** You're telling the PD their attention is a burden. They know. It's the ask that matters.
- **Never send a gift with an ask attached.** The gift is the message.
- **Never share another artist's private contact or a promo rep's list.** The industry is small; leaks travel fast and kill trust permanently.

## THE BURNED-WELL TRIAGE (POINTERS FROM THE REPLAY)

Repair priority is return-on-effort, not guilt:

- **Highest ROI:** the well you *ghosted* (a promise unkept). One honest bridge can recover a full ally in a month.
- **High ROI:** the well you *spammed* (blasted, went quiet). 90-day cooling + clean signal + new value. Slow but recoverable.
- **Medium ROI:** the *clash* well (pitched a mismatch, argued). Only recover if the format changed or a new MD arrived — check the playlist first.
- **Low ROI:** the *value-debt* well (got a yes, gave nothing back). Recover via a genuine giving period — and this one actually *improves* you: it's a discipline lesson, not a repair.

Never re-pitch a well before its cooling period ends. Never re-pitch with the same angle. Two violations and the well is permanent.

## THE INTRO-WEB MULTIPLIER (THE CONFIDANT MOVE)

The highest-value relationship move in the entire system: **introduce two of your contacts who benefit each other.** The PD gets a useful connection, you get network gravity, and the rung you climb is irrelevant — you become a *node of value*. Two intros a month builds a confidant network faster than fifty emails. When your next record drops, the intros you made are the credits you've banked.

## THE ONE SECRET MOST ARTISTS NEVER LEARN

**A PD's time is the only inventory they can't restock — and they notice who respects it.** The 12-minute call, the 3-touch stop, the one-ask rule, and the thank-you economy aren't politeness; they're a *demonstration* that you understand the inventory. That understanding is what makes a PD think of you when a specialty slot opens, a local spot opens, or a new-music hour needs a guest. The relationship isn't the ask. The relationship is the *respect for the clock*.
