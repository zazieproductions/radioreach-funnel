# THE 90-WORD PITCH — Fillable Command Deck

*The only email that matters. Five blocks, 90 words, 4 seconds, one ask. Fill the blanks, count the words, send.*

---

## THE FIVE-BLOCK SKELETON (COPY, FILL, COUNT)

**BLOCK 1 — THE NAME (≈ 10 words)**
I'm [ARTIST], [AGE / CITY], and I make [ONE GENRE NAME].

**BLOCK 2 — THE PROOF (≈ 20 words)**
[ONE third-party receipt with numbers]: [X] local streams, [X] tickets sold, [X] spins, [X] press.

**BLOCK 3 — THE FIT (≈ 25 words)**
[SONG] is a [LENGTH] radio edit that sits in your [FORMAT/SHOW] — [one specific station reason, verified].

**BLOCK 4 — THE ASK (≈ 20 words)**
Would it fit [SHOW / BLOCK / ROTATION]? Happy to send the WAV and a 90-second promo for your board.

**BLOCK 5 — THE LOGISTICS (≈ 15 words)**
EPK: [URL] · radio edit attached · [ARTIST] on all platforms · [PHONE].

**TOTAL: ______ words (90 ± 5)**

---

## THE WORD COUNTER RULE
Paste into any counter. Over 91: cut. Under 60: you're hiding. Revise six times. Read aloud in 30 seconds.

## STATION VARIANT ENGINE (ONE PER TARGET)
Same skeleton, three swapped blocks per station:
- **PROOF block** → that market's receipt (local streams, local tickets, local press).
- **FIT block** → their actual showing/format, verified from their playlist.
- **ASK block** → their actual slot name.
