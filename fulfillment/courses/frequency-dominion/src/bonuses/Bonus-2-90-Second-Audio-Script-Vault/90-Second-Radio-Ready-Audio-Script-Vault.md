# THE 90-SECOND RADIO-READY AUDIO SCRIPT VAULT

*10 complete, voice-ready scripts. Record with the 5-act structure below. Every script fits 90 seconds at a normal broadcast pace (≈130–140 wpm).*

---

## THE 5-ACT STRUCTURE (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

| Act | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:10 | The hook — most undeniable 10 seconds of the song, no intro |
| 2 | 0:10–0:25 | The voice drop — 15 words of spoken intro |
| 3 | 0:25–0:50 | The second hook — the money moment, loudest |
| 4 | 0:50–1:15 | The story voice — date, city, venue, one proof line |
| 5 | 1:15–1:30 | The CTA — one request, one call-to-action |

**Recording rules:** one take per line, record vocals after the hook (pick vocal dynamics to match), master to -14 LUFS, true peak under -1 dBTP, export WAV + MP3, name file `ARTIST-SONG-90SEC`.

## THE HOOK FORMULA BANK (ACT 1 SWAPS)

- **The fact hook:** "This is what [NUMBER] people are hearing right now."
- **The place hook:** "It's happening here first: [CITY]."
- **The moment hook:** "This is the one from the [VENUE] show."
- **The proof hook:** "The one they added at [STATION]."
- **The genre hook:** "The [GENRE] record of [SEASON]."
- **The name hook:** "You know the name. Now the song: [SONG]."
- **The tension hook:** "No label. No playlist deal. Just [NUMBER] stations."
- **The date hook:** "Two weeks before the [VENUE] show — here it is."

## THE CTA FORMULA BANK (ACT 5 SWAPS)

- Request: "Request [SONG] on [STATION]."
- Ticket: "Tickets at [LINK] — [DATE] at [VENUE]."
- Follow: "[ARTIST] on all platforms — link in the show notes."
- Repeat: "Again on [STATION] — [SONG] by [ARTIST]."
- License: "Stems and promo kit at [EPK URL]."
