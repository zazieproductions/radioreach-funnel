# THE 30 SUBJECT-LINE FORMULAS — Cut-Through Noise Vault

*Four engines. 4–7 words. One formula per send. Never ALL CAPS, never "!!", never emojis, never "URGENT" unless the building is on fire.*

---

## ENGINE 1 — THE PROOF (receipts beat adjectives)

1. `[NUMBER] local streams · [your market]`
2. `[NUMBER] tickets sold · [venue] · [city]`
3. `Added at [station] — [your market] responding`
4. `[NUMBER] spins since [month] — [genre]+[market]`
5. `Featured in [press] · [artist] · [city]`
6. `[NUMBER] saves last 30 days · [market]`
7. `[Sister station] added it — [this station] next?`

## ENGINE 2 — THE LOCAL STORY (dates are the strongest magnet)

8. `Local artist · [venue] · [date]`
9. `Hometown show [date] · [artist] · [city]`
10. `[Artist] brings [NUMBER] listeners to [city] [date]`
11. `$[NUMBER] ticket presale · [venue] · [date]`
12. `Before the [date] show — [artist] for [station]`
13. `Your market's local single — [date] release`
14. `[City] artist, [city] story, [date] date`

## ENGINE 3 — THE PROGRAMMER'S INTEREST (you understand their clock)

15. `Fits your [FORMAT] block · 3:12 edit`
16. `Between [Song A] and [Song B] · [your song]`
17. `For your [SHOW NAME] · [artist] · [genre]`
18. `Your 7–9pm local block · [artist]`
19. `[Genre] for [DAYPART] — [artist]`
20. `Late-night slot fit · [artist] · [song]`
21. `Rotation-ready · [duration] · clean edit ready`

## ENGINE 4 — THE HUMAN CONNECTION (name + specificity)

22. `[PD First Name], quick one about [their show]`
23. `[PD First Name] — [station] + [local date]`
24. `Hi [PD First Name], [artist] from [city]`
25. `[PD First Name], saw the [format] shift — [artist]`
26. `[PD First Name] · [song] · [station]`
27. `From [Artist] to [PD First Name] — [NUMBER] words`
28. `[PD First Name], the one you mentioned [topic]`

## DIRECT ASK (use sparingly — one ask, zero drama)

29. `[Station] + [artist] — 60 seconds?`
30. `One slot for [artist]? [market] local`

---

## THE RULES (CLIP TO YOUR DESK)

1. **4–7 words.** Longer dies in the preview pane.
2. **One engine per line.** Two engines = no engine.
3. **Never scream.** No caps, no "!!!", no emojis, no "URGENT".
4. **Never fake.** No "RE:" on a first send, no "following up" as a subject.
5. **Test weekly.** Two formulas per wave; log opens and replies; kill losers.
6. **Specific beats clever.** A verifiable number beats a pun 10 times out of 10.
