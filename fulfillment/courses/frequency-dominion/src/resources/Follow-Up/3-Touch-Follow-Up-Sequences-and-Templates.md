# THE 3-TOUCH FOLLOW-UP SYSTEM — Sequences & Copy-Paste Templates

*Touch 1 = the pitch. Touch 2 = value. Touch 3 = grace close. Then silence. Never repeat an asset. Never "just following up."*

---

## THE CADENCE

| Touch | Day | Job | Asset |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | The 90-word pitch + audio | The weapon |
| 2 | +4 | New proof, new value | The receipt |
| 3 | +8 (or +10 cold) | Grace close | Tiny new asset or none |

---

## TOUCH 2 — THE VALUE TEMPLATE (single receipt, zero reminders)

**Subject:** `[NUMBER] [receipt] since [song] · [their station]`

Hi [PD First Name],

Quick update since my last note: [NEW RECEIPT — e.g. "we sold out [venue] Friday, here's the crowd video" / "700 local streams this week" / "sister station [CALLSIGN] just added it"].

If it makes the decision easier, the 90-sec promo is here, and the clean WAV is ready the moment you ask.

[ARTIST]

*(No "following up." No guilt. The receipt is the message.)*

---

## TOUCH 3 — THE GRACE CLOSE (60 words, door open, zero pressure)

**Subject:** `[song] — one ping, then I'm done`

Hi [PD First Name],

Last note from me on [SONG] — if it's not a fit, no hard feelings at all. If it is, the clean WAV and 90-sec promo are one click away, and I'd love to know for the next single too.

Either way, thanks for your ears. We'll be playing [venue] on [date] — you're welcome anytime.

[ARTIST]

## THE REPLY HANDLING SCRIPTS

### REPLY 1 — "Send it again / send the clean version" (act within 4 hours)
**Subject:** `Here you go — [song] clean WAV`

Hi [PD First Name],

Here's the clean WAV (3:12) and the 90-sec promo. One click, no folder. If it fits, tell me the slot and I'll tell my whole team — this city is going to hear about it.

[ARTIST] · [phone]

### REPLY 2 — "Not right now / wrong time" (set a recheck date, not a rejection)
**Subject:** `Totally get it — [recheck month]`

Hi [PD First Name],

No problem at all. I'll check back in [MONTH] with the next single — and if anything changes before then, I'll keep it to one note. Thanks for the honest answer — rare and appreciated.

[ARTIST]

### REPLY 3 — A real question (answer in under 12 hours, then offer the call)
**Subject:** `Great question — [answer in 2 lines]`

Hi [PD First Name],

[Answer the question directly in 2 lines, no essay.]

If it's easier to talk through, I'm free [DAY] [TIME] — 10 minutes, and I'll bring the single, the WAV, and the [market] numbers.

[ARTIST] · [phone]

---

## THE 3 HARD RULES

1. **Three touches maximum.** Touch 4 does not exist. MONITOR + 90-day recheck.
2. **One new asset per touch.** Repeating the same link is a reminder, and reminders are spam.
3. **Every touch logged.** Date, type, value, outcome — in T4.3 Followup-Tracker.csv.
