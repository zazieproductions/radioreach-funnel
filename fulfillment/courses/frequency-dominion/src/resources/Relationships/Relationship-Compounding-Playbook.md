## THE 3 MOVES (REPEAT EVERY MONTH)

1. **Re-rank** — scan the T5.4 Ally Engine Tracker; move cold→warm→active by 30-day touch windows.
2. **One asset per active ally** — clip, ID, tickets, premiere, note. No ask attached.
3. **Two intros** — connect two people you trust who can help each other. Log both.

## THE 30/10/10 DEFINITIONS

- **ALLY (30+):** has played/replied/met you. Needs a touch within 120 days or it decays.
- **WARM (10):** you've traded assets, had a call, or gotten a real reply. Needs a touch within 60 days.
- **ACTIVE (10):** touched within the last 30 days and ready for the *next* ask. This is your campaign fuel.

## THE REPAIR SCHEDULE (ONE PER QUARTER)

- Q1: repair the well with the **highest potential upside** (biggest market, warmest history).
- Q2: repair the well you **ghosted** (promise broken — highest trust debt).
- Q3: repair the well you **spammed** (blasted list — signal + bridge).
- Q4: repair the well where the **PD changed** (new decision-maker = clean slate attempt).

## THE INTRO SCRIPT (2 SENTENCES, SEND THE SAME DAY)

> "Hey [A], you should know [B] — they [specific value to A]. [B], this is [A], [specific value to B]. I'll let you two take it from here."
