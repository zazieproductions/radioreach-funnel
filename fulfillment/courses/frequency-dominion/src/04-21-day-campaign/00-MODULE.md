# MODULE 04 — THE 21-DAY CAMPAIGN

> A calendar that tells you who to email on which day.

Module 04 is the campaign: Week 0 loads it, Days 1–7 fire it, Days 8–14 follow up, Days 15–21 convert, close, and clone.

| # | Lesson | Spoken | Drill |
|---|--------|:------:|-------|
| 01 | [Week 0: load the war room](01-week-0-load-the-war-room.md) | ~8 min | Run the Week 0 checklist: 20 queued rows, frozen assets, named calendar blocks, open log, briefed humans. |
| 02 | [Days 1–7: first blood sends](02-days-1-7-first-blood-sends.md) | ~8 min | Day 1: send 10 A-list emails, personal first lines, log all 10. Day 3: send the next 10. Day 7: audit. |
| 03 | [Days 8–14: the 3-touch follow-up](03-days-8-14-the-3-touch-follow-up.md) | ~8 min | Draft Touch 2 + Touch 3 today. Execute per calendar: T2 on day 8–10, T3 on day 14, replies same-day. |
| 04 | [Days 15–21: adds, interviews, next market](04-days-15-21-adds-interviews-next-market.md) | ~8 min | Conversion pass (every thread → a written noun), audit numbers into the log, and load market two's war room with Day 1 calendared. |
