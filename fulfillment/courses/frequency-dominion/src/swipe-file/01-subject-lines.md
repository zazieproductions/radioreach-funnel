# SWIPE 01 — Subject Lines That Get Opened

## The logic
Specificity is respect: prove you know the show, the clock, and the city — in under 8 words.
**Die on arrival:** “New single for your consideration” · “Demo attached” · “Checking in” · “Unsigned artist seeking airplay.”

## Templates (fill every bracket)

1. [City] [genre] for [show] — [runtime]
2. Interview window [date range] + “[song]”
3. Local date [venue] [date] — radio edit [runtime]
4. Follow-up: still a fit for [show]?
5. 30-second hook for [daypart] — [Artist]
6. [Station] specialty: “[song]” ([other-station] add last [period])
7. Load-in [city] [date] — live cut + radio edit
8. Replacement link — “[song]” [runtime] (only if a link broke)
9. [First name] — one track, one ask
10. Hometown [genre] for this week’s [show]
11. New info only: [quote / chart / room change] + “[song]”

**Write 11. Keep 3. Never reuse a loser on the same contact.**

## Filled example

1. Cincinnati midnight-synth for The Night Signal — 3:24
2. Interview window Apr 14–16 + ‘Static Bloom’
3. Local date Apr 17 (Foundry on Elm) — radio edit 3:24
4. Follow-up: still a fit for The Night Signal?
5. 30-second hook for Friday night — Marisol Vega
6. 89.1 specialty: ‘Static Bloom’ (3 wks on KCYQ’s Lowlight)
7. Load-in Cincinnati Apr 17 — live cut + radio edit
8. Replacement link — ‘Static Bloom’ 3:24 (first one broke)
9. Dara — one track, one ask
10. Hometown midnight-synth for this week’s Night Signal
11. New info: Queen City Beat quote + bigger room Apr 17

*Kept #1, #5, #11. Every line proves show, clock, or city — in under 8 words.*

> EXAMPLE ONLY — Marisol Vega, every station, every person, every show, and every number below are fictional, built for training. Never copy example stations or “proof” into your own campaign. Real contacts come from your Radio Reach Vault, and your proof lines must be your own true facts.
