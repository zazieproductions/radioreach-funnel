# Immersive renderer

Stereo is decomposed into mid, side, low-frequency, high-frequency, and delayed ambience components. Front speakers receive directional L/R energy; center receives linked mid; surrounds receive decorrelated ambience and high side; heights receive high-passed diffuse energy; LFE receives low-passed mono. Output energy is normalized globally, then verified per channel.

Supported beds: 5.1, 7.1, 7.1.2, 7.1.4, 9.1.6, Sonic Lab 20.4, and validated custom layouts. Synthetic upmixing cannot recover discrete source objects or original height information.
