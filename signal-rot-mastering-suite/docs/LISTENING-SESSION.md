# Subjective listening session

Engine target: `20.7.0-pre-listening`. Mastering and spatial banks are frozen as `1.0-listening`; each preset displays and exports a stable FNV-1a parameter checksum. The exact pre-revision and listening targets are stored under `baselines/`.

Open **AUDITION LAB**, load one musical source in Stereo Master, select candidates, destination, quality, and loudness policy, then generate the set. Candidate letters are cryptographically shuffled in the browser. Monitor switching uses peak-safe, monitor-only gain matching and marks approximate matches with `≈`. Exported comparison WAVs contain the matched monitor versions; normal mastering exports are never modified by A/B gain matching.

Rate tonal balance, low-mid clarity, harshness, transients, depth, center stability, width, fatigue, emotional fidelity, and preference. Notes and ratings are user judgments and are never inferred from fixture metrics. A blinded manifest hides preset/spatial mapping until **Unblind session** is selected.

For valid comparisons, keep the loop region, playback level, quality, and policy constant. Recheck important judgments in mono and side audition. Do not compare Draft against Audiophile as though an algorithmic difference were a preset preference.
