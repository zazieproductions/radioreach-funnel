# Mastering presets

The fifteen primary presets are engineering intentions, independent of output layout. Every clean preset is fixture-tested for true peak, crest factor, limiter reduction, spectral delta, correlation, and low-band side energy. Transparent Master is the least interventionist; High-Definition controls mud and preserves micro-transients; Open Architecture emphasizes separation; Deep Focus and Psychological Horror preserve cinematic negative space; Glass Atmosphere and Crystal Palace protect upper mids while extending the edge field; Electroacoustic Detail minimizes dynamics processing.

Character presets are separately labeled: Tape Ghost, Broken Media, Vinyl Séance, Rust, Degraded Archive, Corroded Signal, and Haunted Transmission. Their deterministic noise, crackle, rumble, wow, flutter, or nonlinear processing never activates from ordinary presets. Analog Womb is the sole primary preset with an explicitly documented 8% transformer blend.

Preset selection never changes the speaker layout or file format.
