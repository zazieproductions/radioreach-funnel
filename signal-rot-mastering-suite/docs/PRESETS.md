# Presets

Stereo presets remain separate from eight immersive routing intentions: Natural Bed, Scoring Stage, Diffuse Installation, Periphonic Field, Front-Focused Cinema, Liminal Surround, Vertical Atmosphere, and Sonic Lab 20.4 Native. Routing presets set center, surround, height, LFE, front/rear, and diffusion behavior. They do not silently change output layouts. Tape Ghost and Broken Media remain opt-in degradation presets.
