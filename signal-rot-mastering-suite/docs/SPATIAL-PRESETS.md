# Spatial presets

Spatial routing is selected independently from mastering and format.

| Preset | Center | Surround | Height | Intent |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Natural Bed | moderate | restrained | low | conventional coherent bed |
| Scoring Stage | supported | broad | subtle | large cinematic stage |
| Diffuse Installation | light | elevated | moderate | gallery environment |
| Periphonic Field | light | broad | broad | experimental 3D field |
| Front-Focused Cinema | strong | conservative | low | intelligible front stage |
| Liminal Surround | moderate | ambiguous | restrained | uncanny rear/side depth |
| Vertical Atmosphere | moderate | restrained | elevated | high-band vertical texture |
| Sonic Lab 20.4 Native | ring-aware | broad | controlled | custom installation routing |

Each preset sets center extraction, surround/height/LFE energy, front/rear balance, and diffusion. Layout selection determines actual speakers and channel order. Per-ring and per-channel trims remain explicit overrides.
