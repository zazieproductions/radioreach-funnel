Radio Promotion for Independent Artists

Independent radio promotion works best when it is treated as research and relationship-building rather than a giant email blast. The goal is not to contact every station. It is to identify stations where your music plausibly belongs and make it easy for the relevant programmer to hear it.

Build the right radio contact list

A useful radio contact list combines station information with enough context to decide whether outreach makes sense. Depending on the station, useful contacts may include music directors, program directors, specialty-show hosts, DJs, and general submission addresses.

Contact quantity matters much less when the list has no relationship to your genre or audience. Start with relevance, then expand.

Pitch music directors and programmers like humans

Programmers receive a large amount of promotional material. A short, specific pitch is easier to evaluate than exaggerated marketing language. State who you are, what the release is, why it might fit, and where the recipient can hear it.

Avoid repeated unsolicited messages, misleading subject lines, and giant attachments. Good radio promotion should look like professional correspondence, not automated spam.

Use geography strategically

Local and regional angles can create legitimate reasons for outreach. An artist's hometown, tour date, local collaboration, event, or audience may make a release more relevant to a particular market. That is different from manufacturing hundreds of nearly identical location pages or emails.

City-by-city research also makes campaigns easier to organize and measure.

What RadioReach is designed to solve

The Radio Reach Vault is designed for the research layer of independent radio promotion: locating and organizing station contact information. Artists still need to evaluate fit, follow station submission policies, and write their own pitches.

The advantage is simple: less time hunting for contact information and more time doing the part of radio outreach that actually requires judgment.
