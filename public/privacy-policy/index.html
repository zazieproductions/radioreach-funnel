Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

RadioReach (“RadioReach,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you visit radioreach.us or purchase a RadioReach product.

Information We Collect

  • Your name and email address when you place an order or contact us.
  • Payment and billing information necessary to process transactions.
  • Purchase, download, and customer-support information.
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited.
  • Information collected through cookies, pixels, and similar technologies.

How We Use Information

  • Process purchases and deliver digital products.
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the website.
  • Measure advertising and website performance.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and technical problems.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Payment Processing

Payments are processed by third-party providers, including Stripe. RadioReach does not directly store complete payment-card numbers. Payment providers process information according to their own policies.

Cookies and Advertising

RadioReach uses cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to understand website activity, measure advertising results, and improve marketing. You may limit cookies through your browser settings.

Meta Pixel

We use the Meta Pixel, an analytics and advertising tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. (“Meta”), on all pages of this website. The Meta Pixel places cookies and collects information about your visit — such as the pages you view, the actions you take (for example, initiating checkout or completing a purchase), your IP address, browser and device information, and identifiers associated with your browser or Meta account — and transmits that information to Meta.

Meta may use this information, on our behalf and for its own purposes, to:

  • Measure the effectiveness of our advertising and attribute purchases to ads.
  • Build audiences so we can show relevant ads to people who have visited this site (retargeting).
  • Show you ads on Facebook, Instagram, and other Meta services or partner websites.

Information collected by the Meta Pixel is processed by Meta in accordance with Meta’s Privacy Policy. If you have a Meta account, Meta may associate this data with your account. You can manage how Meta uses data to show you ads in your Meta Ad Preferences, and you can opt out of interest-based advertising through the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out tool. You can also block the pixel by disabling third-party cookies in your browser settings or by using a browser extension that blocks trackers.

Sharing of Information

We may share information with service providers that help operate the website, process payments, deliver products, provide hosting, analyze traffic, or measure advertising, including advertising partners such as Meta as described above. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Data Retention

We retain information as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Children’s Privacy

RadioReach is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted here with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us through the contact information provided on the RadioReach website.
