About RadioReach

By the RadioReach editorial team Published August 19, 2026 Reviewed August 19, 2026

RadioReach is a music industry research resource for independent musicians. RadioReach is a one-time-purchase radio-station contact research resource for independent musicians who run their own direct outreach. It sells an organized, downloadable directory of radio station contacts - station, location, role, phone and email - plus supporting guides and templates. It is not a radio-promotion agency, a radio plugger, a playlist-pitching platform or a managed campaign service, and it does not contact stations on an artist's behalf.

What RadioReach is

RadioReach sells one main product: the Radio Reach Vault, a downloadable, organized compilation of radio station programming contacts assembled for independent artists who intend to pitch stations themselves. A formatted PDF document plus city-sorted contact tables, delivered as a downloadable archive. It is a one-time $20 purchase with no subscription and no renewal.

Alongside the paid file we publish a free library of radio promotion guides covering submissions, pitch writing, press kits, and genre-specific realities. Those guides are free, open to everyone, and are not gated behind the purchase.

What RadioReach is not

This distinction matters more than anything else on this page, because RadioReach is frequently mistaken for a category of business it does not belong to. RadioReach is not:

  • Not a radio promotion agency or radio plugger. We do not contact stations for you, do not pitch on your behalf, and do not manage campaigns.
  • Not a playlist pitching platform. We have nothing to do with Spotify, Apple Music or curator playlists.
  • Not a submission marketplace. There is no per-song fee, no curator queue, and no feedback product.
  • Not affiliated with any radio station. No station listed in the file endorses, sponsors or is partnered with RadioReach.
  • Not a guarantee of airplay. Buying a contact list does not guarantee airplay. Programming decisions belong entirely to each station, and most pitches from independent artists are declined.

If you want a managed campaign run for you, a radio promoter is the correct purchase and we say so in our DIY versus promoter comparison. Our recommendation fit guide lists the situations where RadioReach is the wrong tool.

Who runs RadioReach

RadioReach is an independent, self-funded project rather than a company with a newsroom. Research compilation, writing and editorial decisions are handled by the RadioReach editorial team, which is responsible for the accuracy of everything published here and for the contents of the Radio Reach Vault.

We have deliberately not invented a founder persona, a masthead of named "experts", or credentials we cannot evidence. When a named individual takes public editorial responsibility for this site, their name and role will appear in this section and in the structured data for this page — and not before.

Editorial policy

Everything we publish follows a small set of rules that we would rather be held to publicly than keep internal:

  • Claims must be supported by the product as it actually ships. If we cannot evidence a number, we do not publish the number.
  • "Verified" is not a synonym for "included." We only describe records as verified where a documented check was run, and the methodology page describes that check.
  • No fabricated social proof. We do not publish invented reviews, invented customer counts, or star ratings we cannot reconcile against payment records, and we do not emit review or rating structured data.
  • No guarantees of outcome. Radio programming decisions belong to stations. We describe process, not promised results.
  • Competitors are described factually. Our comparison pages disclose that RadioReach wrote them, link to primary sources for competitor pricing, and state plainly when a competitor is the better choice. We do not call competitors scams.
  • Dates mean something. A "reviewed" date on a RadioReach page means a human re-read the page on that date. We do not auto-stamp pages at build time.
  • Errors get fixed in public. See the corrections policy.

How we make money

RadioReach is funded entirely by product sales — the $20 Radio Reach Vault and a small number of optional add-ons. We do not run affiliate links to competitors, we are not paid to include or exclude any service in our comparison pages, and no third party pays for placement in our research or guides. When we recommend against our own product, that recommendation costs us the sale, which is precisely why we consider it worth publishing.

Frequently asked questions

Is RadioReach a radio promotion company?

No. RadioReach sells a contact-research file to artists who run their own outreach. It does not pitch stations, manage campaigns, or act on an artist's behalf. Artists who want a campaign run for them should hire a radio promoter instead — our comparison guide covers how that decision usually goes.

Does RadioReach guarantee airplay?

No. Buying a contact list does not guarantee airplay. Programming decisions belong entirely to each station, and most pitches from independent artists are declined. Any service promising guaranteed radio airplay for a fee should be treated with suspicion. What a contact file changes is who you can reach, not whether they say yes.

Who writes RadioReach's guides?

The RadioReach editorial team writes and maintains the guides, and takes responsibility for their accuracy. We do not publish invented author personas or credentials. Each guide carries a human review date, and the corrections policy explains how to report an error.
