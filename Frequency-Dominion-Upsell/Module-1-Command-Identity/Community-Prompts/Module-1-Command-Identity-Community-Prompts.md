# Command Identity — Community Prompts

Engagement prompts for the member group. Post, tag, hold each other accountable. Zero-vibe rule: data or it didn't happen.


### 1.1 The Unheard Tax — What Being Ignored Actually Costs You
1. **Your tax number.** Post your Unheard Tax amount. One line. No explanations, no caveats. The group holds you to it.
2. **The worst thing you've done.** Post one honest example of the Comfortable Blast, the One-and-Done, or the Prettier Project from your own career. Own it.
3. **What would change at 10 adds?** One paragraph: your career 90 days after your tenth radio add. Specific. Concrete.
4. **Accountability check-in.** Who's running Drill 4 tonight? Post a screenshot of your Proof Stack folder with one file in it. Empty folders don't count.

### 1.2 Station-As-Customer — Radio Is a Market, Not a Favor
1. **Your supplier sentence.** Post your revised one-liner. The group votes: supplier or supplicant?
2. **The worst begging word you used.** Post your most embarrassing pre-course pitch line. We've all done it. This is a recovery group.
3. **The benchmark gap.** Post your benchmark table. What are the three charting songs at your target station doing that you're not?
4. **One change you'll make.** Post the single packaging change you committed to in Drill 2.

### 1.3 The Proof Stack — Build the Evidence Vault That Makes PDs Say Yes
1. **Stack count.** Post your receipt count after the hunt. No receipts yet? Your first week's assignment is generating them.
2. **Your strongest receipt.** Link your single best proof item. The group rates its PD-credibility 1–10 and explains.
3. **One receipt you're hiding.** Post the item you almost threw away but actually counts.
4. **The 30-day pile.** Commit publicly: "I will log X new receipts by [date]." The group tracks your streaks.

### 1.4 The No-Spam Oath & Identity Shift — From Beggar to Frequency Owner
1. **The signed oath.** Post a photo of your signed Oath. No signature = no access to Module 2 accountability thread.
2. **The funeral letter.** Post your "Dear person I used to be" letter. The group reads it out loud in their heads.
3. **Your owner statement.** One sentence. The group rips anything with "hope," "maybe," or "please" in it.
4. **Weekly memo check.** Post your first Operating Memo: targets, touches, replies, adds, one fix. Every Monday.
