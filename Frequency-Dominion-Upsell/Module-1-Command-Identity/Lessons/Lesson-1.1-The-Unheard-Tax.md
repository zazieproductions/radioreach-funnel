> The private doctrine. This is the layer you don't publish.

**THE TAX IS A COMPOUND DECAY CURVE — NOT A LINE**

The amateur treats silence as a static state. It is not. Every month without a system, your response rate decays (spam filters learn you, PDs categorize you, competitors fill the niches you were supposed to own). The true cost of month six of silence is not six months of missing spins — it is the *compounded* cost of month one's foregone leverage re-invested, which is why artists who show up late in a market play from behind. Your only honest option is to accelerate the curve in your favor. You cannot opt out of decay. You can only outrun it with compounding.

**THE 3-CHANNEL MODEL — WHERETHE TAX LIVE**

1. **Reach channel** — the number of humans who can hear you without choosing you. Radio is the only passive-reach channel left at scale. Zero adds = zero passive reach. That is the tax.
2. **Revenue channel** — airplay is a *receipt* that converts other assets: tickets, streams, sync placements, sponsorships, licensing. A radio add is the proof-of-demand that makes the next five deals easier.
3. **Leverage channel** — leverage is the ability to make calls that get returned. Radio adds are the most portable leverage an independent artist can hold, because they are third-party, verifiable evidence that someone other than your mother believes.

**THE AUDIT NUMBER IS A WEAPON**

Do not file it. Tape it next to your workspace. When the Prettier Project enemy whispers "wait until the art's done," the audit number is the counter: the art is already done, and the tax is running. When the Comfortable Blast enemy whispers "just send a bulk email at 11pm," the number says: precision, or loss.

**THE ONLY UNFAIR ADVANTAGE IS EARLIER**

You cannot regenerate last year's leverage. Everything in this course is built to make the *next* 90 days compound — because 90 days of system beats 10 years of hope, and the system starts amortizing the moment you start executing.
