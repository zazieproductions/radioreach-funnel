# THE NO-SPAM OATH — Frequency Dominion, Article One

*The line between a frequency owner and a spammer. Signed once. Re-read before every campaign.*

---

## THE SIX ARTICLES

**ARTICLE ONE — I TARGET. I NEVER BLAST.**
Every station I contact is chosen, scored, and armed. No bulk sends. No copy-paste into 18,000 inboxes. The map decides; I execute.

**ARTICLE TWO — I PERSONALIZE.**
Every pitch names the station, the format, the show, and the market. If I cannot write a station-specific line, I am not ready to send.

**ARTICLE THREE — I PROVE BEFORE I ASK.**
My proof stack, EPK, and receipts travel with every ask. Nobody risks airtime on my word alone.

**ARTICLE FOUR — I RESPECT THE CLOCK.**
No Friday afternoon sends. No 2am scheduling. No twelve emails to one inbox. Their time is the inventory I'm asking for.

**ARTICLE FIVE — I FOLLOW UP WITH SYSTEMS, AND I STOP AT THREE.**
Three touches, spaced, escalated with value. Then MONITOR with a 90-day recheck. Persistence without the stench.

**ARTICLE SIX — I LOG EVERYTHING.**
Every send, reply, and add is in the war room, the ledger, or the proof stack. Data or it didn't happen.
