# Target Architecture — Community Prompts

Engagement prompts for the member group. Post, tag, hold each other accountable. Zero-vibe rule: data or it didn't happen.


### 2.1 The Hometown Heat Map — Find the Stations That Can't Say No
1. **Your map count.** Post your heat-map row count after the sprint. Under 60? Run it again.
2. **Your winner zone.** Post your full 20-station list (names only). The group looks for format mismatches.
3. **Your local story.** Post your best local story sentence. The group rates: would a PD feel stupid NOT playing this?
4. **Ring-1 confession.** Which hometown station have you been avoiding, and why? Say it out loud.

### 2.2 Tour Pre-Roll — Manufacture Airplay Demand With Dates
1. **Your date inventory.** Post your next three dates: venue, capacity, market. No dates? Your first job is booking them.
2. **Your pre-roll asset.** Link the 60-second clip. The group checks the 10/10/20/20 structure.
3. **The listener-discount play.** Which station would you partner with for your next date? Post the plan.
4. **Post-show telegram.** Post your last telegram. If you don't have one, post the one you'll send within 48 hours of your next show.

### 2.3 The Format Scoring System — Rank Every Station Like an Analyst
1. **Your top 5 scores.** Post your highest-scoring stations with their scores. The group challenges any score above 80 (suspiciously high = lies).
2. **Your hardest cut.** Post a station you wanted to pitch but the score said no. The group tells you to trust the math.
3. **The 12-second test.** Post the timestamp of your first hook. Are you under 12 seconds or are you dead on arrival?
4. **The fix this week.** One fixable point you're executing. Post the before/after.

### 2.4 The 20-Station War Room — Build the Command Center That Never Sleeps
1. **Your first row.** Post a screenshot of one fully-loaded station row. The group audits: 9 columns, no blanks.
2. **Your ladder count.** Post your status distribution: X loaded, Y wave-1, Z adds. Numbers only.
3. **Your one change this week.** Post the surgical change you committed to and why.
4. **The 20-minute streak.** Who ran the daily drill 7 days straight? Post your streak count. The group checks in every Sunday.
