# 2.2 Tour Pre-Roll — Manufacture Airplay Demand With Dates

> **THE ONE-LINE TRUTH:** A confirmed show date is the single most potent airplay catalyst in independent music — converting an abstract song submission into a time-stamped, ticket-selling community event.

## MASTERCLASS DIRECTIVE & STRATEGIC BLUEPRINT

### Strategic Foundation & Operational Reality
The most difficult hurdle in radio promotion is overcoming programmer inertia. A music director listens to dozens of excellent songs every week. Even when they appreciate your sound, they frequently defer action: *"Great track, let's keep an ear on them for next quarter."*

A confirmed live concert date completely shatters this inertia by introducing a hard **countdown clock**.

When you pitch a station with an upcoming tour date in their market, the entire commercial dynamic flips:
- You are no longer asking them to evaluate your song in an academic vacuum.
- You are offering their listeners an immediate real-world entertainment experience.
- You provide an urgent news hook: the station can conduct an on-air interview, give away ticket pairs to listeners, and brand themselves as the cultural patron behind a vibrant live event.

This methodology is known as **Tour Pre-Roll**. It synchronizes radio outreach with live performance routing on a disciplined, backwards-engineered countdown starting exactly 21 days prior to venue load-in.

### The 21-Day Tour Pre-Roll Timeline
To extract maximum airplay and ticket conversion, execute this strict 4-phase chronological sequence:
- **T-21 Days (The Anchor Pitch):** Deliver your high-value 90-word pitch to the Music Director and specialty show hosts. Lead with the venue name, performance date, and ticket link. Offer a 10-minute in-studio or phone interview during afternoon drive on the day of show, plus two pairs of guest-list tickets for station listeners.
- **T-14 Days (The Asset Follow-Up):** If unconfirmed, deliver a 30-second live acoustic performance clip or a dynamic 20-second crowd video demonstrating real-world audience energy. Remind the programmer of the upcoming local date.
- **T-7 Days (The Ticket / Station ID Drop):** Provide pre-recorded custom station audio sweepers (*"Hey, this is [Artist Name], catch us live this Friday at [Venue], spinning now on [Station Callsign]"*). Secure on-air spin placement during the pre-weekend specialty show.
- **T+2 Days (The Post-Show Crowd Telegram):** Within 48 hours of load-out, email the programmer a high-resolution 15-second crowd video of the audience singing your chorus, thanking the station for supporting local live music. This single gesture cements a lifetime relationship and guarantees immediate adds on your next single.

**HOW TO RUN TOUR PRE-ROLL WITH ZERO EXISTING TOUR DATES**
If you do not currently have an active tour booked, you do not wait. You **manufacture a regional micro-run**. Book two weekend dates within 90 miles of home (e.g., Friday night in your hometown club, Saturday night at a neighboring college town bar).

Even a single two-venue weekend run provides the legitimate, time-stamped news hook required to execute the Tour Pre-Roll sequence.

**THE INTERVIEW TIME-WINDOW STRATEGY:**
When offering an interview to a radio programmer, never ask them to accommodate your schedule. Propose a specific, convenient window that aligns with their broadcast clock:
- *"I will be arriving at [Venue] for soundcheck at 4:30 PM. I can drop by your studio between 2:00 PM and 3:30 PM for a 10-minute live chat and acoustic performance, or patch in via phone at your exact convenience."*
This signals extreme professional consideration and eliminates scheduling friction.
