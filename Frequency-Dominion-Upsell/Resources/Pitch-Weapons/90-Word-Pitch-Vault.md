# THE 90-WORD PITCH VAULT — Five Loaded Campaigns

*Five complete, fillable pitches by situation. Swap the brackets, count the words, run the 30-second test. Every one follows the five-block structure from Lesson 3.2.*

---

## PITCH 1 — THE HOMETOWN LAUNCH (strongest play)

**Subject:** `Local artist · [venue] · [date]`

I'm [ARTIST], from [city], and I make [genRE]. [NUMBER] local streams last month, [NUMBER] tickets sold for [venue] on [date].

[SONG] is a [length] radio edit that sits in your [format/show]. The [city] angle is real — I'm playing the room your listeners fill.

Would it fit [show/block]? Happy to send the radio edit and a 90-second promo for the board.

EPK: [url] · radio edit attached · [ARTIST] everywhere · [phone].

*(88 words with brackets filled — count it.)*

---

## PITCH 2 — THE TOUR PRE-ROLL (dates carry the ask)

**Subject:** `Hometown show [date] · [artist] · [city]`

I'm [ARTIST], playing [venue] in [city] on [date]. [NUMBER] presales, [NUMBER] locals on the list.

[SONG] is [length], clean, radio-loud — built for your [format]. We're bringing your listeners a show, and we'd love the station to be in the room.

Would [song] fit a slot before the [date] show? Audio + EPK links below.

EPK: [url] · 90-sec promo attached · [phone]. [ARTIST] on all platforms.

*(86 words.)*

---

## PITCH 3 — THE PROOF-CASCADE (after a first add)

**Subject:** `Added at [station] — [market] responding`

I'm [ARTIST]. [Sister station] added [SONG] last week — their [market] audience is responding (2,400 local streams since).

[SONG] is a [length] edit for your [format]. If [station] fits their slot, I suspect it fits yours — same region, same listeners.

One slot for [SONG]? I'll send the WAV and a cut today.

EPK: [url] · clean edit attached · [phone].

*(80 words.)*

---

## PITCH 4 — THE COLLEGE / SPECIALTY FLOOR (open-clock formats)

**Subject:** `For your [SHOW NAME] · [artist] · [genre]`

I'm [ARTIST], [city] native, [genre] with a [specific] edge. [NUMBER] monthly listeners, [NUMBER] spins at [station].

[SONG] is [length], fits a [show/block] slot. It's the kind of track that keeps a specialty show alive — new, local, loud.

Would it land on [show/block] next week? Clean edit + promo attached, 60 seconds to check.

EPK: [url] · [ARTIST] everywhere · [phone].

*(84 words.)*

---

## PITCH 5 — THE REPEAT ALLY (pre-warmed, minus volume)

**Subject:** `[PD LAST NAME] — [song] again?`

[ARTIST] here. You added [last song] in [month] — thank you for that. New single [SONG] is out [date].

[length] clean edit, same lane as [last song]. [NUMBER] local streams before release.

One slot for [SONG] if there's room? WAV ready in one click — no folder, no fuss.

EPK: [url] · [phone]. [ARTIST] on all platforms.

*(70 words — short is fine when the relationship is warm.)*

---

## THE ANATOMY (MEMORIZE)

| Block | Job | Words | Non-negotiable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Name | Identify | ~10 | One genre, no influences |
| 2. Proof | De-risk | ~20 | One third-party number |
| 3. Fit | Demonstrate | ~25 | Their format/show, verified |
| 4. Ask | Decide | ~20 | One question, yes-able |
| 5. Logistics | Execute | ~15 | Link + files + contact |

## THE 6-REVISION CHECKLIST (RUN BEFORE EVERY SEND)

☐ 85–95 words ☐ banned words deleted ☐ one genre named ☐ proof is third-party
☐ ask is single and yes-able ☐ read-aloud under 30 seconds ☐ station-specific block present
