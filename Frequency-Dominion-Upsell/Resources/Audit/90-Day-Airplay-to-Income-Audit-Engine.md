## 1. THE AUDIT ENGINE OPERATING PHILOSOPHY

The greatest financial vulnerability of the independent music sector is **unattributed revenue leakage**. An artist releases a single, secures ten radio adds across three regional markets, experiences a 40% surge in live ticket sales, sees a streaming spike on Spotify and Apple Music, and receives an inquiry from a sync licensing agency — yet fails to correlate any of these commercial events to their broadcast radio airplay.

Because they fail to attribute revenue to broadcast airplay, they view radio promotion as an expense rather than a **high-yield distribution engine**.

The **90-Day Airplay-to-Income Audit Engine** is an institutional governance instrument. It binds your daily broadcast monitoring, weekly outreach pipelines, and quarterly financial statements into a single, unified balance sheet.
