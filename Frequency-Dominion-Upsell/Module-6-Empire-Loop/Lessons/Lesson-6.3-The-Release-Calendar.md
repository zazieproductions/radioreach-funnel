# 6.3 The Release Calendar That Dominates Quarters

> **THE ONE-LINE TRUTH:** A release is not an isolated event — it is a 12-week military operation. The artists who own radio run an annual calendar with three armed campaigns a year, backwards-engineered from impact dates.

## MASTERCLASS DIRECTIVE & STRATEGIC BLUEPRINT

### Strategic Foundation & Operational Reality
The most destructive behavior in the independent music ecosystem is the sporadic, impulsive release cycle. An artist records a single, finishes the master on a Wednesday, uploads it to a digital distributor on Friday, posts on social media on Monday, and by week two has exhausted their promotional energy. They spend the next four months in creative hibernation, wondering why their career lacks commercial continuity.

Dominion operators treat the calendar as an **Enterprise Production System**.

You do not "drop singles." You execute **Quarterly Operational Campaigns**. A sustainable, highly profitable independent music enterprise operates on a strict cadence of **three major campaigns per year**, with each campaign structured as a 12-week continuous execution timeline backwards-engineered from an official **Radio Impact Date**.

### Why Three Releases Per Year Beats Monthly Chaos
Digital distribution "gurus" advocate releasing a new single every 30 days to game Spotify recommendation algorithms. For a serious broadcast campaign, this strategy is catastrophic:
- Radio programming departments operate on multi-week evaluation cycles. It takes 14 to 21 days just for a programmer to listen, evaluate, and assign a track to an add meeting.
- If you release a new track every 4 weeks, you abandon your first single just as radio rotation is beginning to build.
- You burn out your station allies with constant, frantic, low-quality submissions.

Three disciplined, fully armed 12-week campaigns per year give each single sufficient runway to achieve full regional saturation, rotation upgrades, tour sync, and revenue harvest.

### The 12-Week Master Operational Timeline
- **Weeks 1 – 2 (The Proof & Asset Build):** Final master delivered (-14 LUFS, -1.0 dBTP), FCC clean edit bounced, 90-second audio weapon and 30-second sweeper created, ID3 metadata stamped, proof stack refreshed.
- **Weeks 3 – 4 (Target Architecture Lockdown):** Hometown Heat Map audited, 60 candidates scored via 100-Point Format Scorecard, 20-Station War Room Cockpit finalized and verified.
- **Weeks 5 – 6 (Weapon Staging & Gate Clearance):** One-Page Command Deck EPK updated, 20 customized 3-layer subject lines pre-drafted, all 7 Load Sequence gates verified green.
- **Weeks 7 – 8 (The Pre-Roll Launch):** Tour dates confirmed and locked in target station markets, venue interview windows scheduled, station ticket giveaways structured.
- **Week 9 (Wave 1 Dispatch & Impact Week):** Wave 1 dispatches Tuesday 9:30 AM local time. Four-hour monitoring window active. Real-time Spinitron tracking initiated.
- **Week 10 (Wave 2 & Touch 2 Escalation):** Wave 2 dispatches Thursday 9:30 AM local time. Wave 1 receives Touch 2 Asset Drop (30-second hook cut + tour dates).
- **Week 11 (Touch 3 & Cascade Loops):** Touch 3 Dignified Exit dispatched to unresponsive targets. First verified adds leveraged into Cascade Expansion loops across sibling stations.
- **Week 12 (The Harvest & Revenue Conversion):** Thank-You Economy protocol deployed, physical station postcards mailed, tour dates executed, revenue logged in Airplay-to-Income Ledger.

**THE DANGER OF THE DECEMBER RATINGS FREEZE**
Never launch an independent radio campaign between December 1st and January 5th.
- During December, commercial and public radio stations enter the "Holiday Freeze."
- Many stations switch to 24/7 holiday music or freeze all playlist changes to protect their fourth-quarter Arbitron ratings books.
- Programming staffs take extended holiday vacations, and music meetings are suspended.

**OPTIMAL ANNUAL LAUNCH WINDOWS:**
1. **Window A (Late Winter / Spring):** January 15 – April 15 (Fresh budgets, new music appetites, SXSW season).
2. **Window B (Early Summer):** May 1 – July 15 (Upbeat programming, summer festival and tour season).
3. **Window C (Fall / Back-to-Campus):** September 1 – November 10 (College radio returns, major fall tour runs).
