# 6.2 Airplay-to-Income — Convert Frequency Into Money

> **THE ONE-LINE TRUTH:** Airplay is not a metric — it's a *receipt* that converts into tickets, placements, syncs, streams, sponsorships, and deals. Most artists get the spins and skip the conversion. That's the entire revenue leak.

**WAR-ROOM NOTES — 6.2 Airplay-to-Income**

**THE RECEIPT CURRENCY: AIRPLAY IS THE ONLY VERIFIABLE DEMAND INDEPENDENTS HOLD**

Streams are private; radio adds are public and verifiable. A music supervisor, a brand, a booking agent, or a label can check a radio add in 20 seconds — which makes it the only proof-of-demand an independent artist can *hand over*. The entire revenue thesis of this course is: build the verifiable demand (radio), then convert it across every lane. Artists who convert frequency into receipts are not competing on taste — they're competing on evidence.

**THE SIX-LANE PRIORITY MATRIX (WHERE TO START)**

Ranked by cash velocity for an independent artist: 1) tickets (fastest), 2) placements (highest per-unit), 3) streams/saves (signals), 4) merch, 5) sponsorship, 6) career deals. If you have a touring market, tickets are the first lane. If you have no touring market, placements are first: the placement package takes 90 minutes and the add-log proof is *unique* — most indie artists can't produce a verifiable spin log. The matrix is an operating priority, not a menu.

**THE PLACEMENT PACKAGE AS A PRODUCT**

A one-page, proof-first placement package (radio log + spin counts + 90-sec cut + stems + contact) is a piece of inventory a supervisor can act on. It's not a press kit; it's a *licensing offer*. The same package converts twice: once for the supervisor, once for the brand sponsor. Build it once. Deploy it every quarter. The 90-minute build cost is recovered by the first placement.

**THE ATTRIBUTION LEDGER IS THE LTV ENGINE**

The course's most important measurement promise is attributable net profit. Without the ledger, every dollar of income is unproven and every campaign's ROI is a guess. With it, you answer the only question that matters to the system: **what did THIS add produce?** The ledger is the proof that the funnel works — and the ammunition for the ascension pitch (Module 6.4) when the artist is ready to scale with you.
