## IMPLEMENTATION DRILLS

- **Drill 1 — The Pipeline Build (30 min).** Set up the five-stage pipeline: capture template, asset folder, deploy checklist, measure log, recycle bin. **Pass standard:** every add has a visible path through all five stages.
- **Drill 2 — The First Conversion (20 min per add).** Take one fresh add and produce all four assets: screenshot, graphic, clip, quote. **Pass standard:** four files, EPK updated, proof stack logged.
- **Drill 3 — The Friday Drop (20 min, weekly).** Create and post the weekly proof drop, and email it to your 10 warm allies. **Pass standard:** one graphic + one email, every Friday, no exceptions.
- **Drill 4 — The Proof-Refresh (10 min, weekly).** Update EPK receipts, pitch proof lines, and war-room statuses Monday morning. **Pass standard:** no proof older than 90 days in any pitch-facing asset.

**WAR-ROOM NOTES — 6.1 Proof Compounding Systems**

**THE PROOF PIPELINE IS THE ONLY DELIBERATE COMPOUNDING MECHANISM**

Radio's compounding happens between campaigns, not within them. The pipeline converts one add into four assets — which means each add multiplies the *pitch strength* of every subsequent send. The amateur's proof decays into memory; the operator's proof re-enters the market weekly. The differential between the two is not talent; it's the pipeline's existence. Build the pipeline once, and every spin forever after is worth five.

**SAME-DAY CAPTURE IS NON-NEGOTIABLE**

A spin logged a week late arrives with a stale timestamp, no screenshot, no link, no specifics. The proof's *specificity* — station, show, slot, moment — is what makes it credible to the next PD. Same-day capture turns an event into evidence. "Later" turns it into a story. Stories don't move add decisions.

**THE 90-DAY PERISHABILITY RULE**

Proof decays perceptually: 90-day-old spins read as "past success," 30-day-old reads as "current momentum," 7-day-old reads as "this is happening now." The refresh cadence (weekly EPK refresh, monthly stack archive) ensures every pitch carries *now* proof — the single strongest signal you can send a PD. Archive the old proof in a "history" folder; it's valuable for press kits, but it never runs a current campaign.

**THE MOMENTUM NARRATIVE (WHY THE DROP WORKS)**

A weekly proof drop is a public *operating narrative*: "this artist ships, grows, and wins, week after week." It works on three audiences at once: PDs (they see a low-risk trending artist), fans (they see a career in motion), and allies (they see a partner worth investing in). The Friday drop costs 20 minutes and produces more third-party narrative than a year of sporadic posts.

**THE FIRST-ADD AS COLD-START BREAKER**

The first add is the only add that's hard; every subsequent add inherits the proof of the previous one. This is why the first campaign's targeting matters so much (Module 2): the first add is the seed of the entire proof tree. Once the first add is banked and converted, the system's growth curve turns exponential — not because the music changed, but because the evidence started compounding.
