# Empire Loop — Community Prompts

Engagement prompts for the member group. Post, tag, hold each other accountable. Zero-vibe rule: data or it didn't happen.


### 6.1 Proof Compounding Systems — Turn Every Spin Into a Weapon
1. **Your last add's four assets.** Post the proof screenshot + quote from one add. The group checks the conversion pipeline.
2. **Your Friday drop.** Post your most recent weekly proof drop graphic. No drop = you're invisible this week.
3. **Your oldest EPK proof.** Post the age of your oldest proof still in your EPK. Over 90 days? Refresh now.
4. **The cold-start breaker.** Post the exact proof line you'll use to convert your first add into the next station's pitch.

### 6.2 Airplay-to-Income — Convert Frequency Into Money
1. **Your lane inventory.** Post your six lanes with one bridge asset each. Empty lanes = missed money.
2. **Your ticket bridge.** Post the 4-step sequence for your next show. The group checks the station's listener-code step.
3. **Your placement package.** Link your one-page placement package. The group evaluates as fake music supervisors.
4. **Your attribution ledger.** Post last week's lane revenue. Zero dollars = post your zero honestly; the group will name the lane to attack first.

### 6.3 The Release Calendar That Dominates Quarters
1. **Your 12-month calendar.** Post your next 3 release dates with their W-8 gate dates. Three columns, all filled.
2. **Your protected blocks.** Post your weekly machine schedule. No recurring blocks = no machine.
3. **Your most missed checkpoint.** Post the checkpoint you historically skip and the fix you're deploying. Honesty pays.
4. **The quarter review.** Post your last release's one-line rollup: adds, replies, revenue. Data only.

### 6.4 The 90-Day Audit & Ascension Engine — Scale to $1M
1. **Your audit score.** Post your 5-column scorecard total and your weakest column. Full honesty or the engine doesn't work.
2. **Your gate status.** Post your stage, your gate numbers, and the exact gap. Numbers only.
3. **Your one column.** Post the one column you're repairing this quarter and the plan. The group holds you to it.
4. **Your graduation memo.** Post your signed next-quarter operating memo: one change, one commitment. This is the course's final assignment.
