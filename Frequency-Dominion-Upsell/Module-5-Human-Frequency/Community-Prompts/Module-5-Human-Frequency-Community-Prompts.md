# Human Frequency — Community Prompts

Engagement prompts for the member group. Post, tag, hold each other accountable. Zero-vibe rule: data or it didn't happen.


### 5.1 High-Value Calls — The 12-Minute Relationship Call
1. **Your call trigger.** Post your strongest current call trigger: reply, add, or milestone. The group helps you draft the opening line.
2. **The listening questions.** Post your two listening questions. The group checks: are they open, genuine, and non-salesy?
3. **Your 12-minute result.** Post the outcome of your last relationship call: one ask, next step, follow-through time.
4. **The call you botched.** Post the story of a call that went wrong and what you'd re-run. The group collects the scars.

### 5.2 The Thank-You Economy — Tiny Gestures, Lifelong Allies
1. **Your most specific thank-you.** Post it. The group checks: specific or generic? Names, times, impact?
2. **Your highest-ROI asset.** Post the asset you delivered to a station. The group steals the format for their own.
3. **The one ask you're planning.** Post your week-5 ask exactly as you'll send it. No stack. One ask only.
4. **Ledger zero-count.** Post your ledger's empty-row count. Zero claims get applause. Anything else gets a deadline.

### 5.3 Burned-Well Repair — The 5-Step Protocol for Salvaged Relationships
1. **The burn audit.** Post your list of burned wells — station names only, no shame. The group reads it as a map of opportunities.
2. **The diagnosis.** Post your burn type for the worst one. The group verifies the type before the fix.
3. **The bridge.** Post your honest bridge script (text, not result). The group checks: own it, no ask, under 60 words?
4. **The walk.** Post one well you're walking from, and your 12-month recheck date. Numbers, not feelings.

### 5.4 Relationship Compounding — The 90-Day Ally Engine
1. **Your portfolio count.** Post: X allies, Y warm, Z active. Blank cells = empty portfolio.
2. **Your asset/ask balance.** Post your best ally's row: asks vs assets. The group flags any drain.
3. **Your next intro.** Post the intro you'll make this month and who connects to whom. The group helps you draft it.
4. **The 90-day beat.** Post your day-14 value drop plan for one active ally. Specific asset, specific date.
