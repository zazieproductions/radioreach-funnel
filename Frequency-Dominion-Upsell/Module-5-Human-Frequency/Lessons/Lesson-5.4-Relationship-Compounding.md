> **THE ONE-LINE TRUTH:** One add is a transaction. Thirty allies is a moat. The 90-day ally engine compounds relationships on a schedule — and a schedule is the only thing that survives your busy life.

## IMPLEMENTATION DRILLS

- **Drill 1 — The Portfolio Build (45 min).** Open `Templates/T5.4-Ally-Engine-Tracker.csv`. Load every relationship you have: past adds, replies, promoters, contacts. Tag each: ally, warm, or active. **Pass standard:** 30+ rows, every row tagged, no blank cells.
- **Drill 2 — The Re-Rank (20 min, monthly).** Re-rank the tiers against recent touches and asset balance. **Pass standard:** the 10 active are genuinely active, and every row has a next-touch date.
- **Drill 3 — The 90-Day Loop Calendar (30 min).** Fill the four beats for the next 90 days: re-add, value drop, check-in, re-rank. **Pass standard:** all 30 allies have dated beats on the calendar.
- **Drill 4 — The Intro Web (2/month, 20 min).** Schedule two introductions per month. **Pass standard:** two connections made, two follow-ups sent, and the ledger updated with the new nodes.

**WAR-ROOM NOTES — 5.4 Relationship Compounding**

**THE 30/10/10 IS A NETWORK-PORTFOLIO THEORY**

In radio, a 30-ally network with 10 warm and 10 active is the minimum viable moat: enough active allies to float a campaign, enough warm allies to activate a 90-day pipeline, enough total allies to survive any single relationship's decay. The portfolio is re-ranked every 30 days because relationships are *decaying assets*: an active ally becomes warm in 60 days of silence, warm becomes cold in 180. The re-rank forces the maintenance. Without it, the moat erodes invisibly — which is why most artists have a list of names and no network at all.

**THE ASSET/ASK BALANCE SHEET (THE ONLY METRIC THAT MATTERS)**

For each ally, track the ratio of asks to assets. A row with 3 asks and 0 assets is draining the account; a row with 0 asks and 2 assets is accruing. The engine's command rule: **no ask without a prior asset** — the asset doesn't have to be big, it has to be *sent*. This single discipline keeps every relationship compounding. Most artists' relationships die of overdraft — they asked twice, gave nothing, and then wondered why the well is dry.

**THE INTRO WEB IS THE NETWORK GRAVITY ENGINE**

Introductions are the only multiplier in a relationship system: they turn your reputation into a *hub* rather than a *node*. Two intros a month creates 24 flows a year; each flow that lands makes the next intro easier. The operator's network compounds faster than their catalog — and when the catalog is finally great, the network is already enormous. Build the web before you need it.

**THE 90-DAY LOOP IS THE ENGINE'S CLOCK, NOT A METAPHOR**

Day 0 re-adds, day 14 value drops, day 45 check-ins, day 90 re-ranks: each beat keeps the network warm without demanding 3 hours a day. The loop also generates the audit data (Module 6.4) — every beat's outcome feeds the proof stack and the growth curve. The loop isn't maintenance. It's the engine's heartbeat.
