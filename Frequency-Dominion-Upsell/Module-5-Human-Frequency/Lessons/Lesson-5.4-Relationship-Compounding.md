# 5.4 Relationship Compounding — The 90-Day Ally Engine

> **THE ONE-LINE TRUTH:** One radio add is a transaction. Thirty lifetime allies is an unbreachable commercial moat. The 90-Day Ally Engine compounds relationships on a calendar schedule that never sleeps.

## MASTERCLASS DIRECTIVE & STRATEGIC BLUEPRINT

### Strategic Foundation & Operational Reality
The fatal flaw of the independent music business is cyclical amnesia. An artist launches a campaign, works furiously for three weeks, lands four station adds, and then disappears into the studio for eight months. When their next record is finished, they re-emerge to discover that programmers have changed jobs, email threads have gone cold, and they are forced to rebuild their momentum from absolute zero.

Dominion operators operate an institutional CRM: the **90-Day Ally Engine**.

A music director who has added your record once is 5x more likely to add your next record than a stranger—*provided you maintain relational continuity between releases*. You do not let relationships decay. You maintain an active portfolio of station champions through a disciplined, low-friction quarterly maintenance cadence.

### The 30/10/10 Relational Portfolio Model
Your active industry network is organized into three distinct operational tiers:
- **30 Lifetime Allies (The Base Portfolio):** Every station programmer, specialty host, college DJ, or booking agent who has ever played your music, replied to an email, or attended a show. Logged in your master ledger with personal notes, format preferences, and station anniversaries.
- **10 Warm Champions (The Mid-Tier Pipeline):** Programmers who have expressed genuine personal appreciation for your sound, conducted an in-studio interview, or added multiple tracks. Maintained with bi-monthly value drops.
- **10 Active Campaign Targets (The Firing Line):** The 10 priority stations currently being worked for your active single or upcoming tour date.

### The 4-Beat Quarterly Maintenance Loop
Every 90 days, execute this four-phase operational loop across your entire 30-member ally base:
1. **Beat 1: The New Release Pre-Flight (Day 0):** Dispatch your 90-word pitch to all 30 allies two weeks prior to general public release, framed as an exclusive advance preview for long-time supporters.
2. **Beat 2: The Mid-Cycle Value Drop (Day 30):** Deliver an unreleased acoustic cut, live soundboard recording, or custom station sweeper with zero commercial ask.
3. **Beat 3: The Check-In & Service Inquiry (Day 60):** Reach out with a brief, warm note: *"Hi Tom — hope the quarterly ratings book treated WMSE well. Any upcoming local programming specials we can support or amplify for you this fall?"*
4. **Beat 4: The Quarterly Portfolio Audit & Re-Ranking (Day 90):** Review the entire 30-member roster. Re-rank allies, retire inactive contacts to cold archives, and promote promising new station relationships into the active Top 30.
