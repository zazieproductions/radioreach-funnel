# 4.3 The 3-Touch Follow-Up System — Persistence Without the Stench

> **THE ONE-LINE TRUTH:** The first email is a knock on the door. The second is proof you are serious. The third is a dignified exit. Over 65% of all independent radio adds occur on Touch 2 and Touch 3.

## MASTERCLASS DIRECTIVE & STRATEGIC BLUEPRINT

### Strategic Foundation & Operational Reality
Amateurs view follow-up emails as awkward, intrusive begging. They wait two weeks, feel guilty, and eventually send a cringe-inducing message: *"Just bumping this to the top of your inbox!"* or *"Did you get a chance to listen to my single yet?"*

This language communicates zero value, demands emotional labor from the programmer, and creates instant resentment.

Dominion operators know that **programmers rely on follow-ups to filter out the tourists**. A music director receives 500 pitches a week; they intentionally ignore cold first-time emails to see which artists actually possess an organized campaign system and which ones disappear forever after one try.

The **3-Touch Follow-Up System** is a disciplined, multi-stage value escalation cadence. It never bumps. It never asks if they listened. Every single touch introduces a **brand-new, high-value commercial asset or proof receipt**.

### The Strict 3-Touch Cadence & Asset Escalation Architecture
- **Touch 1 (Day 1 / Day 3 — The Initial Payload):** The complete 90-word pitch, 3-layer subject line, proof stack, and private streaming link. Sets the commercial baseline.
- **Touch 2 (Day 8 to Day 10 — The Value Drop):** Dispatched 7 to 8 days after Touch 1. Keeps the original email thread intact so the programmer sees context. Delivers a brand-new asset that lowers listening friction:
  - *Copy Structure (Under 45 Words):* *"Hi [Name] — in case it's quicker during your music meeting, here's a 30-second direct hook stream: [Link]. Also confirmed upcoming [Hometown Club] showcase on [Date]. Would love to see this in rotation for [Show Name]."*
  - *Asset Introduced:* The 30-Second Sweeper Hook or 90-Second Audio Weapon, plus a newly confirmed tour date or press quote.
- **Touch 3 (Day 14 to Day 16 — The Dignified Exit & Final Proof Point):** Dispatched 6 to 7 days after Touch 2. Cites new momentum and provides a clean, respectful off-ramp:
  - *Copy Structure (Under 50 Words):* *"Hi [Name] — quick update: [Song Title] was just added into rotation at [Station A] and [Station B] this week. If it's not a fit for [Station Callsign] right now, no problem at all — we'll reconnect on the next release cycle. Keep up the great programming."*

### The Iron Law of the Exit: Stop at Three
If Touch 3 produces no response, **the campaign is closed**. You do not send a fourth email. You do not send a passive-aggressive message. You log the contact as 'Cold / 90-Day Hold' and exit with total dignity. You have established yourself as a courteous, professional operator who respects their boundaries. When you return with your next single in 90 days, your email will be greeted with respect.

**WHY THREADING YOUR REPLIES IS A NON-NEGOTIABLE LAW**
When dispatching Touch 2 and Touch 3, **always reply directly to the sent message thread of Touch 1**.
- Do NOT generate a brand new email with a new subject line.
- When the programmer opens Touch 2, they must be able to scroll down and immediately see your original 90-word pitch, original proof lines, and contact information.
- Threading proves continuity. It shows the programmer that you have a deliberate, multi-week campaign architecture rather than sporadic, disorganized thoughts.
