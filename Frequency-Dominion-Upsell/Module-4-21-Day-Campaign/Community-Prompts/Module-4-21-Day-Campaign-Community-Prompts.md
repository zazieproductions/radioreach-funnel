# The 21-Day Campaign — Community Prompts

Engagement prompts for the member group. Post, tag, hold each other accountable. Zero-vibe rule: data or it didn't happen.


### 4.1 The Load Sequence — Arm Every Station Before You Send
1. **Your gate pass rate.** Post: X of 20 stations at 7/7. No scores under 100% in the group without shame.
2. **Your hardest blocker.** Post the one gate you're stuck on. The group solves it in the thread.
3. **Your firing order.** Post your top 10 load order with one-line rationale each.
4. **The dry run.** Post your friend's result: seconds-to-music, PD questions answered, ask named.

### 4.2 First Sends — Timing, Waves, and the 4-Hour Rule
1. **Your wave grid.** Post your wave-one/wave-two plan with dates. The group checks death-window violations.
2. **The timezone map.** Post your most exotic send-time calculation. Did you catch the 3-hour offset?
3. **Diagnostic logs.** Post your 24-hour outcome table after wave one. Data or it didn't happen.
4. **The lesson learned.** One sentence: what did your first send teach you about your market? Nobody skips this one.

### 4.3 The 3-Touch Follow-Up System — Persistence Without the Stench
1. **Your pre-dated calendar.** Post your touch-2/touch-3 dates for your top 5. Empty dates = empty campaign.
2. **Your best touch-2 value.** Post the new receipt you used. The group rates: reminder or value?
3. **The grace close.** Post your 60-word touch-3 script. The group checks for guilt, threats, or "last chance" language.
4. **The reply you handled.** Post a real reply and how you handled it within 12 hours. Success earns the group's respect.

### 4.4 Adds & Next-Market Expansion Loops — One Station Into a Cascade
1. **Your first add's 72-hour protocol.** Post the thank-you you sent 24h after your add. The group checks the tone.
2. **The three asks.** Post your top-3 cascade asks for your best add. The group rates: casting or favor?
3. **Your scale gate.** Post: current adds, campaign size, trigger for the next size. Numbers only.
4. **The cascade win.** Post one add that turned into two or three more. The group wants the exact path.
