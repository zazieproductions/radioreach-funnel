# 3.3 The One-Page EPK That Commands Attention

> **THE ONE-LINE TRUTH:** Your Electronic Press Kit is not a promotional brochure — it is a single-screen Command Deck designed to give a gatekeeper all critical operational facts in 20 seconds.

## MASTERCLASS DIRECTIVE & STRATEGIC BLUEPRINT

### Strategic Foundation & Operational Reality
The traditional independent artist Electronic Press Kit (EPK) is a catastrophic failure of information architecture. Artists assemble multi-page PDF documents packed with high-resolution 10MB photo collages, extensive poetic philosophical essays on their upbringing, broken YouTube video embeds, and convoluted quote walls.

No working radio programmer, festival talent buyer, or music journalist has ever read a 12-page EPK.

In the Dominion operating doctrine, your EPK is a **One-Page Command Deck**. It is engineered for a gatekeeper operating under extreme time pressure. A music director must be able to glance at your Command Deck and absorb six critical data blocks in under 20 seconds without zooming, panning, or navigating complex sub-menus.

### The Six Mandatory Blocks of the Command Deck
1. **Block 1: Hero Identity & Visual Authority:** A single, razor-sharp, horizontal landscape photograph displaying high visual charisma and distinct artistic styling. Overlay your artist name, hometown metro, and genre classification. Zero cluttered logos or amateur text treatments.
2. **Block 2: The 80-Word Executive Bio:** An objective, third-person executive summary answering three questions: *Who are you? What is your signature sonic proposition? What major commercial or live milestones have you achieved?* Strike all poetic musings.
3. **Block 3: Embedded Broadcast Audio Player:** An immediate, zero-friction audio player featuring your lead radio single prominently at the top, complete with an instant "Download Broadcast Master WAV" button. Include two secondary focus tracks below.
4. **Block 4: The Verifiable Proof Wall:** Four visual receipt badges: station callsigns currently spinning your record, verified quote excerpts from reputable publications, and confirmed ticket sales receipts.
5. **Block 5: Confirmed Live Routing & Tour Dates:** A clean chronological listing of upcoming live dates displaying Date, City, Venue, and Capacity. If routing is regional, clearly highlight hometown dates.
6. **Block 6: Direct Commercial Contact Information:** Direct email and mobile telephone numbers for the artist, management, or booking representative. Eliminate generic contact forms.

## IMPLEMENTATION DRILLS & PASS STANDARDS

- **Drill 1 — The 80-Word Bio Surgery (30 min).** Draft an 80-word executive bio for Block 2 using the template below. Ensure it contains zero empty hype adjectives and at least two verifiable objective facts.
- **Drill 2 — The Single-Screen Layout Audit (30 min).** Assemble your One-Page Command Deck using HTML/CSS or a clean responsive landing page builder. Verify that Blocks 1, 2, and 3 are visible above the fold on desktop and tablet screens.
- **Drill 3 — The Dead Link & Speed Audit (15 min).** Test every single hyperlink, audio player button, and download link on your Command Deck. Page load time must clock under 1.5 seconds on mobile data.
- **Drill 4 — The 20-Second Civilian Comprehension Test (15 min).** Hand your phone with your Command Deck open to a friend or colleague for exactly 20 seconds, then take it back. Ask them: *What genre do I play? Where am I from? What station plays me?* If they cannot answer, redesign for clarity.

**WAR-ROOM DIRECTIVE — 3.3 The One-Page EPK That Commands Attention**

**THE HOSTING ARCHITECTURE: NEVER SEND AN ATTACHED PDF AS AN EPK**
Sending an attached PDF as your primary press kit is a legacy practice from 2008. PDFs render poorly on mobile screens, require pinch-to-zoom navigation, cannot embed live responsive audio players, and consume mobile data allowances.

**DOMINION EPK PROTOCOL:**
1. **The Web-Native Single URL:** Host your One-Page Command Deck at a dedicated clean URL: `artistname.com/epk` or `artistname.com/radio`.
2. **Password-Free Access:** Never put a password on your press kit. A gatekeeper arriving from a cold pitch will immediately close the tab if confronted with a password gate.
3. **Dedicated Radio Focus:** If possible, create a sub-variant at `artistname.com/radio` that places the Broadcast WAV download link and station quote badges directly in the hero block above all other elements.
