# 3.2 The 90-Word Pitch — The Only Email That Matters

> **THE ONE-LINE TRUTH:** A music director reads your pitch for four seconds. The 90-word pitch is an operational payload — delivering maximum technical clarity, proof, and a single frictionless ask.

## MASTERCLASS DIRECTIVE & STRATEGIC BLUEPRINT

### Strategic Foundation & Operational Reality
The default instinct of musicians is to tell their life story. They write sprawling 500-word biographies explaining their childhood musical influences, their spiritual awakening in the recording studio, their creative philosophy, and detailed track-by-track analyses.

A radio programmer will not read an essay. They have 4 seconds of initial visual patience. If your email requires scrolling on a mobile device to locate the listening link or the core proposition, your submission is dead on arrival.

The **90-Word Pitch** is an engineered communication weapon. It strips away all narrative fluff, artistic posturing, and emotional pleading. It delivers an unassailable commercial proposition structured in six modular blocks.

### The Six-Block Modular Anatomy (Strictly Under 90 Words)
1. **Block 1: Personal Salutation & Show Identification (10 words):** Confirms you know the recipient and target their specific show: *"Hi [Name] — caught your Sunday specialty spotlight on [Station]; loved the [Artist] spin."*
2. **Block 2: Immediate Local or Format Rationale (15 words):** States why this record belongs on their frequency this week: *"Targeting your evening indie rotation with our new Chicago release, '[Song Title]'."*
3. **Block 3: One-Sentence Sonic Hook & Run-Time (18 words):** Clean comparative reference and clock fit: *"High-energy post-punk driven by angular guitars (2:56 run-time, 126 BPM, FCC clean broadcast mix)."*
4. **Block 4: The Undeniable Proof Receipt (17 words):** Concrete third-party validation: *"Currently in rotation on WNUR; headlining Empty Bottle on October 14th (250 cap, 80% advance)."*
5. **Block 5: Frictionless Streaming Link (8 words):** One dedicated private streaming link with zero download walls: *"Stream & Broadcast WAV: [Short Private Link]"*
6. **Block 6: The Single, Low-Risk Ask (14 words):** One single call to action — never ask for three things at once: *"Open to considering this for next week's specialty add or an upcoming show feature?"*

### Total Word Count: Exactly 82 Words.
Notice what is absent:
- No requests for their phone number or general life feedback.
- No demands that they add you to heavy daytime rotation immediately.
- No 50MB attachments that crash corporate inboxes.
- Just clean, respectful, surgical B2B communication.

## IMPLEMENTATION DRILLS & PASS STANDARDS

- **Drill 1 — The Brutal 90-Word Redaction (30 min).** Take your existing email pitch. Paste it into a word counter. Aggressively strike every extraneous adjective, autobiographical sentence, and duplicate link until the entire text sits under 90 words.
- **Drill 2 — The Out-Loud 15-Second Cadence Test (10 min).** Read your revised pitch aloud at a normal conversational pace. You must be able to complete the entire reading in under 22 seconds without stumbling.
- **Drill 3 — The Single Ask Alignment (10 min).** Audit your closing sentence. Ensure it makes exactly ONE ask (e.g., specialty show consideration OR interview window). Delete any compound requests.
- **Drill 4 — The Mobile Visual Hierarchy Check (15 min).** Send the draft pitch to your personal smartphone. Verify that the greeting, sonic hook, proof line, and streaming link are 100% visible on a single screen without finger scrolling.

**WAR-ROOM DIRECTIVE — 3.2 The 90-Word Pitch — The Only Email That Matters**

**NEVER ATTACH AUDIO FILES TO COLD TOUCH 1 EMAILS**
One of the fastest ways to guarantee your email is never seen is attaching an MP3 or WAV file to your initial email. Enterprise mail servers for universities, public broadcasters, and commercial radio networks automatically quarantine incoming emails with external attachments over 5MB or from unverified domains.

**THE OPERATOR'S LINK ARCHITECTURE:**
1. **The Clean Private Link:** Use a single, custom, clean private link (e.g., a branded private Soundcloud, DISCO, or clean Dropbox link).
2. **The Stream + Download Hybrid:** Ensure the destination page allows the programmer to stream the track instantly with one click *and* provides a direct "Download High-Res WAV" button with complete broadcast metadata.
3. **Never Use Expirable File Transfers:** Sending a WeTransfer link that expires in 7 days is amateur suicide. Programmers frequently review candidate queues 10 to 14 days after initial receipt. When they click an expired WeTransfer link, your submission is permanently dead.
