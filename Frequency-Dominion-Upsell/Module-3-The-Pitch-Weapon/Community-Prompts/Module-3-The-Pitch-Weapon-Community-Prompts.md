# The Pitch Weapon — Community Prompts

Engagement prompts for the member group. Post, tag, hold each other accountable. Zero-vibe rule: data or it didn't happen.


### 3.1 Subject Lines That Cut Through Noise — The 30-Formula Vault
1. **Your worst subject line ever.** Post it. Public shaming builds immunity.
2. **Your best open.** Post your highest-lift subject line and the result (open/reply). The group steals it for their own market.
3. **The 4–7 word rule.** Post 3 of your subject lines. The group counts the words and checks the engines.
4. **The lab results.** Post your A/B results: formula vs formula, opens, replies. Data only, no vibes.

### 3.2 The 90-Word Pitch — The Only Email That Matters
1. **Your 90-word pitch.** Paste it. The group counts the words out loud and names the block that's missing.
2. **The word count war.** Post your before/after: your old pitch word count vs your new 90. Shame is data.
3. **The one ask.** Post your single ask. The group checks: is it one, or is it a grocery list?
4. **The 30-second test.** Link your read-aloud recording. The group ranks: PD-ready voicemail or fan prayer?

### 3.3 The One-Page EPK That Commands Attention
1. **Your current EPK body count.** How many EPK versions do you have right now? Post the number. If it's more than one, that's the problem.
2. **The 60-second test.** Link your EPK. The group answers the five PD questions out loud — under a minute or you rework it.
3. **Your genre line.** One sentence. The group checks: defensible to a PD, or an art-school puzzle?
4. **The live photo.** Post your best stage photo. The group votes: receipt or wish?

### 3.4 Radio-Ready Audio — The 90-Second Weapon That Forces Replies
1. **Your three-file package.** Post the three filenames. The group audits: named, loud, clean, under 10MB?
2. **The 12-second test.** Post your hook timestamp. Under 12:00 or you're dead on arrival.
3. **The delivery test.** Post your friend's time-to-music result. Under 60 seconds = weapon. Over = rework.
4. **The friction score.** Score your current delivery 0–10 and post it. The group tells you what to fix first.
