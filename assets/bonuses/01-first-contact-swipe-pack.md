# The First-Contact Swipe Pack

**A Radio Reach Vault founder bonus.**
Seven pitch emails, twelve subject lines, and the four sentences that get you deleted.

---

## Before you send anything: the 90-second rules

1. **One station, one email.** Never CC. Never BCC a list. A program director can
   always tell, and it is the fastest way to get your domain filtered.
2. **Subject lines under 50 characters.** They are read on a phone, in a hallway,
   between two other things.
3. **Three sentences in the body.** Who you are, why *this* station, what you're
   asking for. Anything longer is a press release, and press releases get archived.
4. **One link.** A streaming link — Spotify, Bandcamp, SoundCloud, YouTube. Not an
   attachment, not a Dropbox folder, not a password-protected private page.
5. **Never attach an MP3 to a first email.** It trips spam filters and eats their
   inbox quota. Offer the WAV; send it when they ask.
6. **Use their name.** It is the entire reason you bought the Vault. Use it.

## Swipe #1 — The local artist (your highest-response pitch)

> **Subject:** Local [CITY] artist — new single for [SHOW NAME]
>
> Hi [FIRST NAME],
>
> I'm [ARTIST NAME], a [GENRE] artist based here in [CITY] — I've been listening to
> [SHOW NAME] for a while and I think my new single "[TRACK]" would sit well next to
> what you played [RECENT ARTIST/WEEK].
>
> It's [LENGTH], [ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION], released [DATE]. Here it is: [LINK]
>
> Happy to send the WAV, a clean edit, or an interview if it's useful.
>
> Thanks for listening,
> [NAME] · [PHONE] · [WEBSITE]

**Why it works:** the first eight words establish you're local and that you actually
listen. Both are rare, and both are checkable.

## Swipe #2 — The tour route (send 3 weeks before the date)

> **Subject:** Playing [VENUE] on [DATE] — track for you
>
> Hi [FIRST NAME],
>
> I'm [ARTIST NAME] ([GENRE], from [HOME CITY]) and I'm playing [VENUE] in [CITY] on
> [DATE]. I wanted to send you the single ahead of the show in case it's a fit for
> [STATION]: [LINK]
>
> If you'd ever want a session, a phoner, or ticket giveaways for the show, I can make
> any of those happen.
>
> Thanks,
> [NAME] · [PHONE] · [WEBSITE]

**Why it works:** you're offering the station something — local relevance, a giveaway,
a live segment — rather than only asking. Send this one first; it has the highest hit
rate of anything in this pack.

## Swipe #3 — College radio

> **Subject:** [GENRE] submission — [ARTIST NAME], "[TRACK]"
>
> Hi [FIRST NAME],
>
> I'm [ARTIST NAME], a [GENRE] artist from [CITY]. Sending "[TRACK]" for consideration
> for rotation — it's [LENGTH] and it's [FCC-CLEAN / has a clean edit available].
>
> Stream: [LINK]
> Full info + downloadable WAV: [LINK]
>
> Let me know if you need a physical copy or an ID for the station.
>
> [NAME] · [PHONE]

**Why it works:** college and community MDs need to know three things immediately —
genre, whether it's FCC-clean, and whether they can get the file. Answer all three
before they ask. Offering a station ID is a small thing that DJs genuinely appreciate.

## Swipe #4 — The specialty show

> **Subject:** For [SHOW NAME] — [GENRE] from [CITY]
>
> Hi [FIRST NAME],
>
> [SHOW NAME] is one of the only places I've heard [SPECIFIC SUBGENRE] programmed
> properly, so I wanted to send this directly to you rather than through the general
> submission page.
>
> "[TRACK]" — [ONE LINE ABOUT IT]: [LINK]
>
> No pressure either way. Thanks for the show.
>
> [NAME]

**Why it works:** specialty hosts choose their own music and are usually delighted that
someone noticed the show exists. This is the single most underrated contact type in the
Vault.

---

## Swipe #5 — Follow-up #1 (day 4)

> **Subject:** Re: [ORIGINAL SUBJECT LINE]
>
> Hi [FIRST NAME] — just floating this back up in case it got buried. Single is here if
> you get a minute: [LINK]
>
> Either way, thanks.
> [NAME]

Reply to your own original email so the thread stays intact. Two sentences maximum.

---

## Swipe #6 — Follow-up #2 (day 11, with something new)

> **Subject:** Re: [ORIGINAL SUBJECT LINE]
>
> Hi [FIRST NAME] — small update: [NEW THING — added to a playlist / picked up by
> another station / video is out / show announced].
>
> Still would love to get it in front of you: [LINK]
>
> [NAME]

Never follow up empty-handed twice. The second touch needs a new fact attached.

---

## Swipe #7 — The re-pitch (next release, same contact)

> **Subject:** New one from [ARTIST NAME] — [TRACK]
>
> Hi [FIRST NAME],
>
> You passed on "[OLD TRACK]" last [SEASON] — completely fair. New single is out
> [DATE] and I think it's a lot closer to what [STATION] actually plays: [LINK]
>
> Thanks for the ear either way.
> [NAME]

**Why it works:** acknowledging the pass without a hint of resentment is what makes you
a professional rather than a nuisance. This email starts more real relationships than
any first contact.

## Twelve subject lines that get opened

1. `Local [CITY] artist — new single`
2. `Playing [VENUE] [DATE] — track for you`
3. `[GENRE] submission — [ARTIST], "[TRACK]"`
4. `For [SHOW NAME] — [GENRE] from [CITY]`
5. `New [GENRE] from [CITY] — 3 min listen`
6. `Station ID + new single, if useful`
7. `[CITY] artist, clean edit available`
8. `Fan of [SHOW] — sending one track`
9. `Single out [DATE] — [CITY] local`
10. `Interview + live session offer — [ARTIST]`
11. `Re: [ORIGINAL SUBJECT]` *(follow-ups only)*
12. `New one from [ARTIST] — think this one fits`

**Never use:** all caps, `!!!`, `NEW SINGLE ALERT`, `Check this out`, `Hi there`,
`Opportunity`, or the word `blessed`.

---

## The four lines that get you deleted

| Don't write | Write instead |
|---|---|
| "To whom it may concern" | "Hi [FIRST NAME]," |
| "I know you're busy, but…" | *(delete the sentence entirely — it apologises for existing)* |
| "Please listen to my song and let me know what you think" | "Here it is if it's a fit: [LINK]" |
| "I've attached my album (14 tracks, 62MB)" | "Happy to send the WAV if it's useful." |

---

## Send-day checklist

- [ ] Contact's first name is spelled correctly
- [ ] Station name and show name are correct — **and are actually that station's show**
- [ ] One streaming link, tested in a private browser window
- [ ] Under 120 words total
- [ ] Phone number in the signature
- [ ] Sent between Tuesday and Thursday, 9am–noon their local time
- [ ] Logged in your Send Tracker (Bonus #3) with a follow-up date

---

*Targeted, relevant, human outreach only. Do not send identical mass emails — it's
spam, it's against the Vault's terms, and it doesn't work.*
