# The Station-Fit Filter & Send Tracker

**A Radio Reach Vault founder bonus.**
18,020 contacts is a liability until you know which 40 are worth tonight.

---

## Part 1 — The 5-question fit test

Run this on any station in under 20 seconds. One point each. **Pitch anything scoring
4 or 5. Queue the 3s. Skip 0–2 entirely.**

| # | Question | Point if… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Format match** — do they program your genre at all, even on one specialty show? | Yes |
| 2 | **Independent-friendly** — college, community, public, or specialty programming? | Yes |
| 3 | **Named human** — do you have a music director, program director, or host by name? | Yes |
| 4 | **Geographic tie** — your hometown, a tour city, or a market you can claim a connection to? | Yes |
| 5 | **Reachable** — a direct email, not a general `info@` or a web form? | Yes |

**The honest math:** a 5/5 station is worth more than fifty 2/5 stations. If you pitch
40 stations that score 4+, you are doing better outreach than an artist who blasts
4,000 — and you will still be welcome at those stations next year.

---

## Part 2 — Priority order

Work these tiers in order. Do not skip ahead; each tier builds the proof that makes the
next tier answer you.

### Tier 1 — Hometown (start here, always)
Every station within ~50 miles of where you live, regardless of size. You are not a
stranger here, you are the local act. This is the warmest outreach you will ever do,
and "local artist" is a genuine editorial hook that programmers actively need.

### Tier 2 — Tour route
Every station in every city you have a confirmed date in, pitched **three weeks
before** the show. You're offering local relevance, a possible session, and ticket
giveaways. This tier converts best because you're giving as much as you're asking.

### Tier 3 — Format match
Stations and specialty shows that program your exact subgenre anywhere in the country.
Smaller reach, far higher hit rate. Specialty hosts pick their own music, which means
one person's yes is the whole decision.

### Tier 4 — Regional expansion
Markets adjacent to wherever you've already been played. Now you have a real sentence
to open with: *"[STATION] added it last month."* Airplay is proof, and proof travels.

### Tier 5 — Major market
Only after tiers 1–4 have produced something. Major-market stations are buried in label
submissions; walking in with regional airplay is the only version of this pitch that
isn't a lottery ticket.

## Part 3 — Session plan (one focused night)

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Filter the Vault to your city / target market |
| 0:10–0:25 | Run the fit test, build a list of 20 stations scoring 4+ |
| 0:25–0:35 | Fill your name, track, and link into Swipe #1 or #2 (Bonus #1) |
| 0:35–1:20 | Send 20 emails — personalise the first line of every single one |
| 1:20–1:30 | Log all 20 in the tracker with a day-4 follow-up date |

**One night. Twenty real decision-makers.** That's the entire method.

## Part 4 — The send tracker

Print this, or paste the header row into any spreadsheet.

```
DATE   STATION        CITY        CONTACT         ROLE   FIT  T1  T2  T3  T4  STATUS    NOTES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                                                        /5
```

### Status codes

| Code | Meaning | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| `SENT` | Pitch is out | Follow up day 4 |
| `BUMPED` | Touch 2 sent | Follow up day 11 with a new fact |
| `UPDATED` | Touch 3 sent | Close on day 25 |
| `CLOSED` | Touch 4 sent | Nothing — wait for next release |
| `REPLIED` | Human responded | **Reply within 24 hours.** Always. |
| `ADDED` | In rotation | Thank them, and tell tier-4 stations about it |
| `NO` | Declined | Re-pitch next release — this is a warm contact now |
| `STOP` | Asked not to be contacted | Remove permanently. No exceptions. |

### Weekly review — five minutes, every Monday

1. Who is due a follow-up this week?
2. Which `REPLIED` rows have I not answered? *(Answer them first. Always.)*
3. Do I have a new fact for anyone at touch 3?
4. Which `ADDED` stations can I cite when pitching tier 4?
5. What's my reply rate — replies ÷ stations contacted?

**A realistic benchmark:** targeted, personalised outreach to well-matched stations
tends to produce single-digit to low-double-digit reply percentages. Anything above
zero beats a submission form that never writes back. Track your own number over three
releases and you'll know exactly which tiers deserve your nights.

---

## Part 5 — The three mistakes that waste the Vault

1. **Starting with the biggest stations.** They're the hardest yes and the most crowded
   inbox. Start where you have an actual edge, which is home.
2. **Sending 200 emails in one night.** Personalisation collapses somewhere around
   email 30. Twenty good emails beat two hundred generic ones — every time, and it
   isn't close.
3. **Not tracking sends.** Without a tracker you'll double-pitch someone who already
   passed, forget the follow-up that would have landed, and never learn which tier is
   actually working for your music.
